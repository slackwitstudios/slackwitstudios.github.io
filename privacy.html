Nova Kiln Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Slackwit Studios

The short version
  • Nova Kiln has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • We (Slackwit Studios) don't run servers that collect your information, and we don't sell it.
  • Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. They come from Google AdMob, which collects some device information. Where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

Nova Kiln is a game made by Slackwit Studios, a one-person game studio. In this policy "we" and "us" mean Slackwit Studios. If you have a question about your privacy, email us at slackwithstudios@gmail.com.

2. Information kept on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in a file on your device: your worlds, upgrades, research, in-game currencies, skins, and the settings you choose. It also records which store orders have already been delivered, so a purchase is never given twice. This file stays on your device. We don't receive it.

Uninstalling the game deletes this file. Settings → Reset also clears your progress, although items you bought in the Star Shop stay yours.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Nova Kiln shows rewarded ads only. An ad plays only when you tap a button that says so, for example to double the Dust you earned while away. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. If you buy No Ads, those rewards are given instantly and no ads are shown.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

InformationWhat it's used for
Your device's advertising ID and app set IDShowing and measuring ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud
IP address, which can show your approximate location (city or region)Choosing relevant ads and preventing fraud
How you interact with ads, such as views and tapsMeasuring ads and preventing fraud
Performance and diagnostic information about the ad softwareKeeping ads working reliably

This information goes directly to Google, encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and anywhere else the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before any personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. That button appears wherever the choice applies.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • No ads at all. Don't tap the "watch ad" buttons, or buy No Ads. Everything in the game can be enjoyed without watching an ad.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy Glint (an in-game currency) and No Ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your name, card number or other payment details. To deliver a purchase, Google Play tells the game which item you bought, an order number and whether the payment went through. The game keeps order numbers only on your device, so each purchase is delivered once. No Ads can be restored from the Star Shop on a new device that uses the same Google account.

Purchases are handled inside the game by Unity's in-app purchasing software, which passes this purchase information between Google Play and the game. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We don't use analytics services to track you, and we've switched off the Unity engine's diagnostic reporting.
  • We don't sell your personal information or share it with data brokers.

6. Children

Nova Kiln is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, email us and we'll help.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR in Europe or state laws in the United States, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. We keep no personal information about you on our own systems. Your game data stays on your device, and you can delete it by uninstalling the game. For information held by Google, including advertising data, use your Google Account and Google's privacy tools. If you have any request or question, email us and we'll answer within 30 days.

8. Security and retention

Your game data stays on your device for as long as the game is installed. Information collected by Google's ad software is sent encrypted and kept according to Google's own retention policies.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
