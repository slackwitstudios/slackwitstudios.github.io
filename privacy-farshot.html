Far Shot Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Slackwit Studios

The short version
  • Far Shot has no accounts of its own. Your progress is saved on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google keeps a copy of your progress in your Google Account (cloud save).
  • The free demo shows ads from Google AdMob: full-screen ads between rounds, and optional ads you can watch for a reward. Google's software collects some device information to do this. Where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We (Slackwit Studios) don't run servers that collect your information, and we don't sell it.

1. Who we are

Far Shot is a long-range shooting and ballistics game made by Slackwit Studios, a one-person game studio. In this policy "we" and "us" mean Slackwit Studios. If you have a question about your privacy, email us at slackwitstudios@gmail.com.

2. Information kept on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in a file on your device: your career, ranks, medals, in-game currencies (Brass and Tungsten), Energy, purchases, rifle set-ups and the settings you choose. It also records which store orders have already been delivered, so a purchase is never given twice. This file stays on your device. We don't receive it. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy is also kept there (see section 5).

Uninstalling the game deletes this file.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free demo shows two kinds of ads: regular full-screen ads after a finished event, and optional rewarded ads that play only when you choose them, for example to refill Energy. Ads are never shown while you aim or shoot. The full game, the Supporter pack and Remove ads stop the regular ads. Optional rewarded ads stay available.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

InformationWhat it's used for
Your device's advertising ID and app set IDShowing and measuring ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud
IP address, which can show your approximate location (city or region)Choosing relevant ads and preventing fraud
How you interact with ads, such as views and tapsMeasuring ads and preventing fraud
Performance and diagnostic information about the ad softwareKeeping ads working reliably

This information goes directly to Google, encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and anywhere else the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • No regular ads. Buy the full game or Remove ads. Everything in the game can be played without watching an optional ad.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy the full game, Remove ads, the Supporter pack, the Firing Solution pack and packs of Tungsten (an in-game currency). Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your name, card number or other payment details. To deliver a purchase, Google Play tells the game which item you bought, an order number and whether the payment went through. The game keeps order numbers in its save file on your device so each purchase is delivered once. Purchases can be restored on a new device that uses the same Google account.

Purchases are handled inside the game by Unity's in-app purchasing software, which passes this purchase information between Google Play and the game. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

5. Google Play Games (cloud save)

Far Shot can save your progress with Google Play Games, which is optional. If you sign in, Google signs you in with your Play Games profile and keeps a copy of your progress in Saved Games, linked to your Google Account, so you can carry on on another phone. The copy holds the same data as the save file described in section 2. It is sent encrypted and stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and it is kept until you delete it. The game doesn't receive your email address or contacts. You can delete the cloud copy by deleting Far Shot's progress from your Google Account (see Manage game progress linked to your Google Account) or in the Play Games app.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking service.
  • We don't sell your personal information or share it with data brokers.

7. Children

Far Shot is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, email us and we'll help.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR in Europe or state laws in the United States, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. We keep no personal information about you on our own systems. Your game data stays on your device (and, if you use cloud save, in your Google Account's Saved Games), and you can delete it by uninstalling the game and deleting Far Shot's progress from your Google Account. For information held by Google, including advertising data, use your Google Account and Google's privacy tools. If you have any request or question, email us and we'll answer within 30 days.

9. Security and retention

Your game data stays on your device for as long as the game is installed; a cloud save stays in your Google Account until you delete it. Information collected by Google's ad software and Google Play is sent encrypted and kept according to Google's own retention policies.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
